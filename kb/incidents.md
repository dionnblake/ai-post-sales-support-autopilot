# Incident and security handling

## Service outage report
For a service outage or multiple users unable to work, collect the affected workspace ID, approximate start time, and visible error. Route to Technical Support with critical urgency. Do not promise a restoration time or invent a service-status update.

## Security or unauthorized access
Reports of unauthorized access, exposed credentials, data exposure, or suspicious account activity must go directly to the Security queue for a specialist. Do not draft troubleshooting instructions or disclose account details. Never ask for passwords, access tokens, or one-time codes.
