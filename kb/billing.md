# Billing and subscriptions

## Download an invoice
Workspace owners can download invoices from Settings > Billing > Invoices. Select the billing month and choose Download PDF. Members without the owner role must ask their workspace owner for the invoice.

## Duplicate charge or refund request
For a duplicate charge or refund request, send the invoice ID and charge date to the Billing queue. A billing specialist reviews eligibility. Support must not promise a refund, change a subscription, or request full payment-card details in a ticket.
